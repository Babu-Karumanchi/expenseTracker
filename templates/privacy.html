Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We have designed Spendly to handle only the data we need to provide a personal expense tracking experience — and nothing more.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the service, Spendly collects the following categories of information:

Account information. When you create an account, we collect a display name, an email address, and a password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plaintext). These let us identify you, keep your records associated with your account, and get in touch when needed.

Expense data you provide. The expense entries, categories, amounts, dates, and descriptions you log in Spendly are stored so we can show them back to you, generate summaries, and support filtering and review.

Usage and device information. We may collect limited technical information — such as browser type, device type, IP address, and timestamps of access — for security, debugging, and improving the service. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will remove the account and its data.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect only to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use it to:

Provide the core service — saving, retrieving, summarizing, and filtering your expense records when you sign in.

Authenticate you and keep your account secure, including detecting and preventing unauthorized access.

Communicate with you about service updates, security notices, or responses to your support requests. We do not send marketing email unless you have explicitly opted in.

Diagnose issues, understand aggregated usage patterns, and make the product more useful. Where we analyze usage data, we do so in the aggregate or in a form that does not identify you personally.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers operated by reputable infrastructure providers. We use industry-standard safeguards — encrypted transport (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access-controlled databases — to help protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.

No method of transmission or storage, however, is 100% secure. While we work hard to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for using a strong, unique password for your Spendly account.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information and expense records from active systems within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law (for example, for tax or bookkeeping records).

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of third-party providers to run the service. These may include:

Hosting and infrastructure providers that store our database and serve the application to your browser. These providers process data only on our behalf and under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

Email delivery providers that send account and security emails (such as password resets) on our behalf.

Analytics providers that help us understand aggregated, de-identified usage of the service so we can prioritize improvements.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or in response to a valid legal request from a competent authority. We will limit any such disclosure to what is strictly necessary.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You have control over your data. You can view, edit, or delete individual expense entries at any time through the app. You can close your account entirely by contacting us; doing so will remove your personal information and expense records from active systems, subject to the retention exceptions noted above.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or to ask us to correct data that is inaccurate. To exercise any of these rights, reach out via the channels listed below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will provide a more prominent notice — such as an in-app message or an email to your registered address — before the new terms take effect.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise your data rights, or need to report a privacy concern, please reach out through the support channels listed in the Spendly application.
